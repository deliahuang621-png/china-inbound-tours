How I work

Who I am

I'm Delia Huang — a Chinese itinerary designer, 16 years into study-tour products, and I have led the groups myself, in both directions: taking students abroad and hosting school teams coming to China. I know this country from the inside, and I work with you in professional English — no translation chain. That's what my itineraries are built around: your guests' safety first, and clients who come home happy.

I'm the bridge between the two sides of this business: I understand what your market needs, and I turn it into day-by-day plans that licensed Chinese inbound operators can actually deliver. Your clients are received in China by those licensed operators; you sign and pay the operator directly, and hotels, transport, guides, insurance and site bookings all sit under that operator's license and name.

What you get from me

  • The real China — your clients came for China, not for a checklist. Day-by-day plans that take them past the famous sights into the country itself.
  • Reachable in English — a question about an itinerary, a booking, a change: one email to me, usually same-day. No translation chain, no general inbox, no ticket numbers.
  • Custom-built for your market — an itinerary built for another market rarely fits yours. Trip length, modules and pacing adjusted to your clients' calendar and expectations — or take one of my fixed itineraries as-is.

Working principles

  • B2B only. I work with tour operators, educational travel companies and group organizers — never individual travelers.
  • Non-exclusive. I work with operators across multiple markets and multiple licensed ground operators.
  • Reliable — no unverified claims. Anything not yet confirmed with a ground operator — prices, hotel grades, site availability — is marked as a placeholder in every document I send. You will never see a number I can't stand behind.
  • Policy before product. If the visa logic doesn't check out for your market, I won't pitch you the itinerary.
  • Legal entry only. I only build trips for travelers who can enter China legally — through whatever channel their nationality requires.
