How I work

Who I am

I'm Delia Huang, an individual itinerary designer specializing in China inbound travel. I come from six years in international education, where I learned the part of this industry that decides whether a tour happens at all: whether the visa policy actually lets your clients in — for long enough, through the right port of entry.

I know what your product team is up against. Policies that change mid-season. Source markets that each need their own answer. School boards and district offices asking questions no brochure answers. I read the Chinese primary sources — National Immigration Administration policy interpretations, port lists, regional rules — so you don't have to, and I translate them into product decisions: which route, which policy, which season, for which of your markets.

And when a school or educational travel company asks for a study-tour version of a route, I build that too — the school group brief covers staffing ratios, safety documentation and the study-activity rules of each policy.

What you get from me

  • Route concepts — day-by-day itineraries with the visa logic solved first: policy, day count, port of entry, permitted geography.
  • Customization — changing trip length, swapping modules, or splitting a route to fit your market's school calendar or holiday windows.
  • Policy monitoring — visa rules in China have been moving fast. I re-verify sources and flag anything that affects itineraries you've already sold.

What you get from my operator partners

Ground execution: hotels, transport, site bookings, guides, safety management, insurance, quotation and invoicing — everything contracted and delivered by a licensed Chinese inbound tour operator, the role the trade calls a receptive operator or DMC. You sign with them directly; your contract and payments sit with the operator, not with me.

Working principles

  • B2B only. I work with tour operators, educational travel companies and group organizers — never individual travelers.
  • Non-exclusive. I work with operators across multiple markets and multiple licensed ground operators.
  • No unverified claims. Anything not yet confirmed with a ground operator — prices, hotel grades, site availability — is marked as a placeholder in every document I send. You will never see a number I can't stand behind.
  • Policy before product. If the visa logic doesn't check out for your market, I won't pitch you the route.
