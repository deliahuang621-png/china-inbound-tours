How I work

Who I am

I'm Delia Huang, an individual itinerary designer specializing in China inbound travel. I come from six years in international education, where I learned the part of this industry that decides whether a tour happens at all: whether the entry policy actually lets your clients in, for long enough, through the right gate.

I read the Chinese primary sources — National Immigration Administration policy interpretations, port lists, regional rules — so your product team doesn't have to. Every itinerary I publish states which visa-free policy it runs on and links the official source.

What you get from me

  • Route concepts — day-by-day frameworks with the visa logic solved first: visa policy, day count, port of entry, permitted geography.
  • Customization — day-count changes, module swaps, segment splitting for your market's school calendar or holiday windows.
  • Policy monitoring — visa rules in China have been moving fast; I re-verify sources and flag what changes for your sold products.

What you get from my operator partners

Ground execution: hotels, transport, site bookings, guides, safety management, insurance, quotations and invoicing — everything contracted and delivered by a Chinese inbound tour operator holding the required inbound reception license (the role the trade also calls a receptive operator or DMC). You sign with them directly — your contract and payments sit with the operator, not with me.

Working principles

  • B2B only. I work with tour operators, educational travel companies and group organizers — never individual travelers.
  • Non-exclusive. I work with operators across multiple markets and multiple licensed ground operators.
  • No invented facts. Anything not yet confirmed with a ground operator — prices, hotel grades, site availability — is marked as a placeholder in every document I send. You will never see a number I can't stand behind.
  • Policy before product. If the visa logic doesn't close, I won't sell you the route.
