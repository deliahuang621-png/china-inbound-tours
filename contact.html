Every inquiry is answered by me personally — no ticketing system, no sales team. For the fastest useful answer, include:

  1. The itinerary name (or your theme: STEM, food, heritage, photography…)
  2. Your source market / clients' nationalities
  3. Preferred season and trip length
  4. Group size and traveler type (school group, adults, families)

Not sure whether your clients can enter China visa-free? Just ask — tell me their nationalities and I'll confirm the entry requirements for each.

What happens next

  1. I send the complete itinerary proposal, with entry rules matched to your clients' nationalities.
  2. If it fits, I connect you with a licensed Chinese inbound operator for ground services, quotation and contracting. Before you sign, you receive the operator's license and insurance documents to verify in your own name.
  3. We customize the itinerary to your market together.

International tour operators, educational travel companies and group organizers only, please.
