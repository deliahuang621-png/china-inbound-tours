How it works

  1. You review the itinerary — day-by-day framework, target segment, entry rules, and the selling logic for your market.
  2. I connect you with a licensed Chinese inbound operator for ground services, quotation and contracting. You sign with them directly.
  3. I stay on the product side — tailoring the itinerary to your market, adjusting trip length, and keeping it correct as conditions change.

I work with international tour operators on a non-exclusive basis — and with more than one licensed inbound operator in China.

No China contact? That's exactly who I work for.

The partners I work best with want to sell China but have no one in China: no Chinese-speaking staff, no trusted ground operator, and no appetite for emails that take days to answer. What you get is a person in China:

  • Answered fast, in professional English. One email reaches me personally — usually the same day. No ticketing system, no translation chain, no nuance lost.
  • A working line into licensed operators. I know which licensed Chinese inbound operator fits which itinerary, so quotation and contracting start in days, not after months of cold emails to strangers.
  • One accountable person, before and after the sale. I stay involved from first proposal to final delivery. If conditions change mid-season or an itinerary needs reworking, you reach one person who knows your product — not a general inbox.
