China currently runs five visa-free policies relevant to group travel — unilateral 30-day waiver (50 countries), 240-hour transit (57 countries, including the US, Canada and Mexico), Hainan 30-day (61 countries), and two Hong Kong / Macao group policies with no nationality restriction.
