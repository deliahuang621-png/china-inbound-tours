How it works

  1. You brief me — your market, your clients, and what you want to sell.
  2. I design the itinerary — day-by-day framework, target segment, entry rules, and the selling logic for your market. You review it before anything goes further.
  3. I connect you with a licensed Chinese inbound operator for ground services, quotation and contracting. You sign with them directly.
  4. I stay on the product side — adjusting trip length, adding or swapping modules, and keeping it correct as conditions change.

I work with international tour operators on a non-exclusive basis — and with more than one licensed inbound operator in China.
