The Visa-Free Playbook Official-source verified

China currently runs five visa-free policies relevant to group travel — unilateral 30-day waiver (50 countries), 240-hour transit (57 countries, including the US, Canada and Mexico), Hainan 30-day (61 countries), and two Hong Kong / Macao group policies with no nationality restriction. Which policy fits which nationality, how many days it allows, and which of the 65 designated ports it uses — all in one page, all with links to the official NIA sources.
