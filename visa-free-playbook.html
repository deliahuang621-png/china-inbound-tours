China Visa-Free Playbook for Tour Operators

Official-source verified 2026-10-01 Sources: National Immigration Administration (NIA) of China, policy interpretations dated 2026-02-17 and 2026-08-20. Links at the bottom of this page.

Five visa-free policies decide who can sell what into China right now. An itinerary is only bookable if its geography, day count and client nationality all fit within one of them. This page is the working reference — written for product teams, not lawyers.

The five policies

ChannelEligible nationalitiesMax stayAllowed areaKey conditions
A. Unilateral 30-day waiver
(NIA, 50 countries)		 50 countries — 35 in Europe, 7 in Asia, 6 in the Americas, 2 in Oceania.
Full country list: available on request.		 30 days (counted from 00:00 the day after entry) Nationwide, all purposes incl. tourism, business, exchange visits A regular passport — "ordinary passport" in NIA's official English. (Diplomatic and service passports follow different rules.) The default policy for A-list nationalities.
B. 240-hour transit waiver
(NIA, 57 countries)		 57 countries — 40 in Europe, 9 in Asia, 6 in the Americas, 2 in Oceania.
Full country list: available on request.		 240 hours (10 days) Enter via any of 65 designated ports in 24 provinces; move freely within the permitted areas across those provinces Confirmed onward ticket (fixed date + seat) to a third country or region — returning to the departure country does not qualify. Hong Kong and Macao count as third regions. Study, work and news reporting are excluded. School study tours cannot use this policy.
C. Hainan 30-day waiver
(NIA, 61 countries)		 61 countries — 39 in Europe, 14 in Asia, 6 in the Americas, 2 in Oceania.
Full country list: available on request.		 30 days Hainan province only Tourism, business, visits, family, medical, exhibitions, sports. Work and study excluded.
F1. HK/Macao group → Guangdong
(NIA, no nationality restriction)		 Citizens of any country with diplomatic relations with China 6 days All of Guangdong province; enter/exit as a group (2+) organized by a HK/Macao-registered travel agency through open ports of the 10 eligible cities Group must travel as a unit (group in, group out). The only fully visa-free policy for US student groups into Guangdong.
F2. HK/Macao group → Hainan
(NIA, no nationality restriction)		 Same as F1 6 days Hainan province only Same group mechanics as F1. Backup policy for short Hainan products.
G. Mutual exemption treaties
(MFA consular service list)		 29 treaty partners across Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa and Oceania.
Full country list: available on request.		 30 days (max 90 days per 180) Nationwide For G-list nationalities this stacks with every other policy.
Deliberately not used in any of my concepts: ad-hoc reliance on the 24-hour transit rule, and any port list not published by NIA. Every policy above links to an official source below.
